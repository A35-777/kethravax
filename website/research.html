ACADEMIC FOUNDATIONS

Prior-Work Audit & Scientific Gap Analysis

KETHRAVAX does not operate in a vacuum. The code/data separation problem has over a decade of established academic research. This audit maps existing milestones and demonstrates the unaddressed capability gap solved by KETHRAVAX.

1. From Linear-Chain CRFs (ELISA) to Recurrent Models (Remigio et al.)

ELISA (Polino et al.): Formulated fine-grained code/data separation as sequential tagging using Linear-Chain Conditional Random Fields (CRFs) paired with logistic regression for ISA elicitation. While statistically sound, the CRF inference penalty ($O(T \cdot |Y|^2)$) and fragility on packed binaries limited its scalability.

Remigio et al. (IWSEC 2023): "The Good, the Bad, and the Binary: An LSTM-Based Method for Section Boundary Detection in Firmware Analysis." Replaced ELISA's hand-crafted CRF with a Bidirectional LSTM network and entropy-based packed architecture classification, demonstrating superior recovery of macro section boundaries on IoT firmware images.

The Remaining Gap: Both systems strictly tackled a 2-class binary distinction (CODE vs DATA) at the macroscopic section level, suffering from boundary localization smearing (±64 bytes) and inability to segment internal structures like jump tables, string literal pools, or alignment padding.

2. Whole-File & Block-Level Classifiers: Magika, FiFTy, and cpu_rec

Magika (Google 2024): A highly optimized 1D CNN for fast content-type identification. Crucially, Magika is a whole-file classifier; it assigns one file-type label to an entire file. It cannot segment contiguous sub-regions within a heterogeneous stream.

FiFTy (Mittal et al.): Compact CNN classifying isolated 512B or 4KB disk sectors across 75 file types. Lacks continuous sequence modeling and boundary localization.

cpu_rec (Airbus Seclab): Statistical n-gram frequency corpus matching. Highly effective over large sliding windows (4KB–64KB) for identifying CPU architectures, but completely blind to non-executable data structures and fine-grained transitions.

3. The KETHRAVAX Unified Formulation

KETHRAVAX unifies these fragmented capabilities:

1. Dense Multi-Class Taxonomy: Replaces binary code/data with 7 verifiable structural classes (Code, Strings, Jump Tables, Raw Data, Padding, Opaque, Ambiguous).
2. Mixed-ISA Stream Support: Decouples the structural category from the ISA label, enabling detection of architecture changes (e.g. ARM32 to Thumb-2) in a single stream.
3. Exact Byte Snapping: Dual-head neural modeling paired with local gradient/null-discontinuity snapping guarantees exact byte offset boundaries.
