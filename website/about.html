About KETHRAVAX

Created and maintained by Arin Goel (@A35-777).

Research Motivation

In binary analysis and reverse engineering, container headers (like ELF section headers or PE data directories) are an artifact of the compiler and linker. When analyzing bare-metal IoT firmware, kernel memory injections, or corrupted disk images, those headers simply do not exist.

Existing tools either rely on coarse file-carving signatures (which miss embedded structures) or basic binary code/data classifiers. KETHRAVAX was created to provide a scientific, reproducible, multi-class segmentation engine that outputs exact byte-offset boundaries with calibrated uncertainty.

Core Engineering Principles

  • Zero Cloud Telemetry: Reverse engineering security researchers often inspect sensitive, classified, or proprietary payloads. KETHRAVAX runs 100% locally on CPU. No data ever leaves your device.
  • Headerless by Design: Operates strictly on raw byte sequences. Does not assume or require magic bytes.
  • Calibrated Uncertainty: Expresses honest confidence scores rather than hallucinating high certainty on short or ambiguous byte ranges.
  • Strict Boundary Invariant: The segment sequence guarantees total byte coverage from offset 0 to EOF without overlapping, duplicate, or skipped bytes.
