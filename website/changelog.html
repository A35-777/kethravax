v0.1.0-BETA — Initial Research Release

November 2026

Initial open-source release of KETHRAVAX, introducing learned multi-class structural byte segmentation and ISA discovery for raw, headerless binary streams.

Key Capabilities:

  • 7-Class Structural Taxonomy: Discrete classification for CODE, DATA_STRING, DATA_TABLE, DATA_RAW, PADDING, COMPRESSED_OPAQUE, and UNKNOWN.
  • Multi-ISA Support: Architecture discovery across x86-64, x86-32, ARM32 (ARM/Thumb), AArch64, MIPS32, and RISC-V.
  • Exact Boundary Snapper: Dual-head model paired with null-discontinuity gradient snapping achieving 0.914 Boundary F1 at τ=4B.
  • Interactive Web Explorer: Zero-cloud client-side reverse engineering workbench with synchronized hex dump, visual byte minimap, and diagnostics.
  • Python SDK & CLI: Full kethravax analyze and kethravax inspect command-line tooling.
