ACADEMIC FOUNDATIONS & AUDIT

Prior-Work Audit & Gap Analysis

KETHRAVAX builds upon over a decade of established binary analysis research. This audit maps existing milestones and demonstrates the unaddressed capability gap solved by KETHRAVAX.

1. From Linear-Chain CRFs (ELISA) to Recurrent Models (Remigio et al.)

Early research like ELISA employed Linear-Chain Conditional Random Fields (CRFs) to detect code/data boundaries in firmware. While theoretically grounded, CRFs suffered from quadratic inference cost over large sequences.

Remigio et al. (2021) pioneered Bidirectional LSTMs for section boundary detection in firmware analysis. However, their scope was strictly binary: classifying bytes as either CODE or DATA, without identifying embedded jump tables, literal string pools, or multi-ISA boundaries.

2. Where Existing Tools Fall Short

  • Binwalk: Relies on magic byte signatures. Completely fails on raw stripped machine code or embedded jump tables lacking container markers.
  • cpu_rec: Sliding-window n-gram frequency counter. Classifies the dominant architecture of an entire file, but cannot isolate boundaries between mixed structures.
  • Google Magika: State-of-the-art whole-file AI content type classifier. Outputs a single MIME type for a file, rather than a contiguous sequence of internal structural segments.
